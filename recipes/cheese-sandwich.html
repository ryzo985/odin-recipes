Cheese Sandwich

Description

Make a delicious grilled cheese sandwich with almost no effort!

Very tasty.

Ingredients

  • 2 pieces of bread
  • Slice of cheese
  • Butter
  • Sandwich grill

Steps

  1. Slather the pieces of bread with butter.
  2. Put a slice of cheese between the two.
  3. Grill the sandwich with a sandwich grill
