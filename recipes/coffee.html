Coffee

Description

Make a hot cup of coffee!

Energizing!

Ingredients

  • Instant coffee powder
  • Boiling water
  • Milk
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. Put 1tsp of coffee powder in a cup
  2. Pour 40ml of boiling water in it and mix with a spoon
  3. Pour 1tsp sugar, mix
  4. Pour 200ml milk, mix
